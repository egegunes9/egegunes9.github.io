🛡️ GuardRush: Gate Defense — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Summary

GuardRush does not require an account and does not ask for personal information such as your name or email. Your progress is stored only on your device. Starting with version 1.1, the app shows ads via Google AdMob (see below).

Advertising

The app shows ads via Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may process information such as device identifiers (IDFA, if you grant permission), approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data. See the Google Privacy Policy for details.

Users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland are shown a GDPR consent form and may decline personalized ads. On iOS, Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission is requested; if you decline, ads are shown without personalization. You can change tracking preferences anytime in iOS Settings > Privacy > Tracking. You can permanently remove interstitial ads with the "Remove Ads" purchase in Settings.

In-App Purchases

The "Remove Ads" purchase is processed entirely by Apple's App Store. We never receive your payment details.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, your scores and achievements are submitted to Apple to power leaderboards and achievements. This data is handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. The app itself does not receive or store other players' personal information.

Local data

Your progress (gold, items, mastery, settings) is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
