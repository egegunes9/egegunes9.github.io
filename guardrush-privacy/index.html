🛡️ GuardRush: Gate Defense — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Summary

GuardRush does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics SDKs, and no advertising SDKs in this app.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, your scores and achievements are submitted to Apple to power leaderboards and achievements. This data is handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. The app itself does not receive or store other players' personal information.

Local data

Your progress (gold, items, mastery, settings) is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
